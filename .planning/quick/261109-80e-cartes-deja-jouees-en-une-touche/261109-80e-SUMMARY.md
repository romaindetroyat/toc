# Quick task 261109-80e: « Les cartes jouées », in one tap Summary

Romain wanted to see quickly which cards have already been played in this pack (« As 2, Roi 3 »). The sheet from 261109-6an now counts the other way round: for each of the 13 ranks it shows how many of the 4 cards were already played since the last shuffle, dead hands included. One tap on the défausse pile on the felt opens it, on the phone and on the computer.

## What was built

- **Engine** (`unseen.ts`): `playedCards(view)` returns the discard pile plus the dead hands laid down in this deal, once each, in deck order. `deckCount` gains `played` (per rank) and `playedTotal`. The code confirms that cards played on the felt go into `state.discard` (`movedState`), that `endDeal` moves the dead hands there with no duplicates, and that the reshuffle empties it. `unknownCards` and the bots are unchanged.
- **Tests** (`unseen.test.ts`, 6 new): nothing played in a fresh pack; counts rank by rank in deck order; a dead hand counts at once and only once (also when the same card is in both places, and after `endDeal`); the own hand and the card passed to the partner never count; 0, then 20, then 36 over the three deals, back to 0 after the reshuffle. A last test plays whole games through the engine (4 seeds, up to 9 deals, crossing reshuffles) and checks at every step, for every seat, that the count matches an oracle built from the `cardPlayed` / `handDiscarded` / `reshuffled` events, and that for every rank played + hidden + own hand + the card the partner holds = 4.
- **Sheet** (`DeckSheet.svelte`, `paquet.ts`): titled « Les cartes jouées ». Head line « Donne 2 sur 3 de ce paquet · 10 cartes jouées » (also « aucune carte jouée » and « 1 carte jouée »). It shows 13 tiles in a 7 + 6 grid: the short name, a large digit and 4 dots, filled for the played cards. As, 7, Valet and Roi have a border and a bold name. Under the grid comes the quieter « 26 cartes au talon. », then the explanation: since the last shuffle, dead hands included, your hand and the card passed to your partner are not counted, everything goes back to zero when the pack is reshuffled. Screen-reader lines read « As : 2 jouées sur 4 », « 5 : aucune jouée », with U+00A0 before the colon. Names and digits are larger when the screen is at least 600 px tall.
- **One tap** (`Board.svelte`): a new invisible button `[data-defausse-button]` sits over the défausse pile. It is named and titled « Cartes jouées », with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, and is at least 44 x 44. It gets the focus back when the sheet closes. It also lets taps through to the knights on boards under 314 px, like the felt button. The rest of the felt still opens « Sur le tapis ».
- **« Sur le tapis »**: the « Cartes jouées » button is now the first element, before the seat rows, so it is above the fold on the phone (screenshot `quick-jouees-tapis-844x390.png`).
- **« T », the info-row button and the keyboard help** are now « Cartes jouées » / « Ouvrir les cartes jouées. ».
