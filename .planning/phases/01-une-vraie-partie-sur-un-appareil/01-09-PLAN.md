# Phase 1 Plan 09: As et Roi, le 7 réparti, le Valet, et une partie entière au doigt

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** faire une partie complète de TOC contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés, **so that** je retrouve une vraie partie de TOC fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, avec l'ambiance du plateau original.

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan every card is playable through the UI: the As/Roi chooser, the step-by-step 7 splitter with undo, the Valet in two taps with its swap preview, and the tray that resolves overlapping knights on small phones. A complete game is played to the end through taps only. Runs in parallel with plan 01-10 (motion) — disjoint files.

<objective>
Extend the turn reducer and the game UI with the As/Roi mode chooser (PLAY-04), the Valet two-tap flow with swap preview (PLAY-02), the 7 splitter (PLAY-03, D-17) and the disambiguation tray, then prove a full game through the UI and the 44 px touch targets on small screens.

Purpose: success criteria 2 and 3 complete (every card, every special flow, a full game).
Output: turnUi extension + tests, ActionZone/Overlay/HitLayer updates, PawnTray, SevenPanel, StepChip, three e2e specs.
</objective>
