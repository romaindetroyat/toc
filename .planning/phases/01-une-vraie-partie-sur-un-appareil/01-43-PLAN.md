- expect.legal, expect.reason, expect.after;
- expect.legalInclude and expect.legalExclude, checked against legalActions(state, seat), the 7's plans included;

- « Décidé » lists D-01 to D-04.

tools/render-scenarios.mjs draws every fixture into docs/regles/<id>.webp, deterministically: a re-render leaves existing pictures byte-identical. A scenario with no `after` is drawn twice: the moves that must be allowed are ringed in green, the moves that must be refused in red.
