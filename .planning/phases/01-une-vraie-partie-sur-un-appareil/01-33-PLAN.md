# Phase 1 Plan 33: Compacter l’écran sur téléphone pour agrandir le plateau C+

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 3 and 4, fifth of eight plans)

After 01-30 to 01-32 the 2:3 board fills the height left by the chrome of 01-24 (info bar 56, action zone 120, hand 136), which leaves it only about 228 px wide at 390 x 664 (the emulated iPhone 13 viewport, zero insets). On 2026-10-14 Romain chose « Compacter l'écran »: a thinner info bar, the action buttons on the hand's row (no separate action-zone band on a phone in portrait), a hand a little lower and shorter, keeping the 44 px targets, the copy, the focus order, the keyboard hints, the 7 assistant and the tray. 01-29 wrote the contract in 01-UI-SPEC.md (« Écran compact (téléphone en portrait) ») and logged the decision in 01-CONTEXT.md (D-07); the dock's details and numbers are the planner's and go to Romain's end-of-phase re-test (01-36).

Budget behind the targets (planning computation, the plan checker's finding included): the regular fan's box is the card height + 36 px (`.fan` in HandFan.svelte), which would be 126 px with 64 x 90 cards and leave the board at about 283 px; so in the dock the fan's box must be exactly the 92 px hand row, with the cards clipped at the bottom. Chrome = 44 + 56 + 92 = 192 px from 375 px wide, 44 + 56 + 48 + 92 = 240 px below; board width = (screen height − chrome − insets) / 1.5466 (1.5 plus the head room), capped by the width less the two 16 px gutters: 390 x 664 → ≈ 305 px (305 x 458, ring knights about 18 px, cells about 19 px apart: +22 % for the knights, +13 % for the cells against the former square board, +34 % for the board width against the 228 px of 01-30); 375 x 667 → ≈ 307; 390 x 844 and 390 x 763 → 358 (width-limited); 320 x 480 → ≈ 155. Landscape is unchanged: its board height is the screen height less the 16 px outer margin above and below (32 px in all, none on a screen 400 px high or less) and its width at most the screen width less 368 px (the 320 px panel, the 16 px gap and two 16 px outer margins): 480 x 320 → 112, 1280 x 800 → (800 − 32) / 1.5466 ≈ 497.

Safe-area insets assumed: every asserted size is Playwright's Chromium emulation, whose env(safe-area-inset-*) are 0. Real devices (computed, re-tested by Romain in 01-36, not asserted): an iPhone 13 installed as a standalone app has insets of 47 px at the top and 34 px at the bottom on its 390 x 844 screen, which leaves 763 px: the board is width-limited at 358 px, target held; in Safari with its toolbars shown the insets are assumed 0 (≈ 305 px); should Safari report the 34 px bottom inset there, the board would be ≈ 283 px, under the 300 px target.

Purpose: Romain's « Compacter l'écran »: C+ readable on his iPhone (UAT tests 3 and 4).
Output: shared height properties, the dock in ActionZone (prompt row, hand row with the button column), the 44 px info bar, the compact fan (64 x 90 cards in a 92 px box), the tray and the 7 assistant in the dock, e2e/compact.spec.ts with the rendered heights and the compact targets, the whole suite green.
</objective>
