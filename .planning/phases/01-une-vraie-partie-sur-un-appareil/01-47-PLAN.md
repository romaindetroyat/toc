Design of the compact mode (Game.svelte's CSS, the contract 01-48 copies into 01-UI-SPEC.md):
- `--strip: 60px` and `--prompt-row: 24px`.
- `--board-size: max(0px, min(100vw − 2 × var(--strip) − var(--inset-x), (100dvh − var(--prompt-row) − var(--inset-y)) / var(--board-ratio)))`.
- Head room: none of its own, because the prompt row is the room. Set `--head-room: 0` for the stage in this mode; a lifted top-row knight's head may rise into the prompt row.
- Grid: three columns. The two strips are each `max(var(--strip), (100vw − var(--inset-x) − var(--board-size)) / 2)` wide, so the board is centred and the strips take what is left. Two rows: the prompt row, then the rest. The strips span both rows; the stage centres the board in its cell.
- Left strip, top to bottom:
  - the info pill (52 px wide, 44 px high);
  - the free space;
  - the hand column, bottom-aligned: cards 52 × 73, overlapped so that each card shows its top 44 px (its index), the last card whole. Five cards take 4 × 44 + 73 = 249 px. The selected card moves 6 px toward the board and keeps its amber outline. The received card keeps its amber outline (« Reçue » in its accessible name). Unplayable cards are at 40 %.
  - During the 7, the step chips of the selected knight replace the hand in a column (44 × 44, 4 px apart, scrolling vertically when they do not fit). During « Lequel ? », the A–D chips (44 × 56) replace them.
- Right strip, top to bottom:
  - « Réglages » (44 × 44);
  - « Avance rapide » (44 × 44, icon only, aria-pressed, accessible name « Avance rapide », title « Avance rapide »);
  - the free space;
  - the state's buttons, bottom-aligned, stacked 4 px apart: 52 px wide, at least 48 px high, Label 14 bold on two lines at most, one primary at most, which is the lowest.
- Short visible labels in this mode only. The accessible name and the title keep the full label:
  - « Annuler ce coup » → « Annuler »;
  - « Changer de carte » → « Autre carte »;
  - « Choisir un autre cheval » → « Autre cheval »;
  - « Passer cette carte » → « Passer »;
  - « Poser mes cartes » → « Poser »;
  - « Sortir un cheval » → « Sortir »;
  - « Avancer de n » → « +n »;
  - « Jouer quand même » → « Jouer » (the warning stays in the prompt row);
  - « Retirer le dernier pas » → « Retour »;
  - « Tout annuler » → « Effacer »;
  - « Jouer » unchanged.
- Prompt row: the consigne, the bots' last move and the 7's « Reste n » with its plan, in Label 14 crème, on one line centred over the board. A longer text ends with « … », its full text stays in the aria-live region and in the title.
- Info pill: a button showing the turn dot (amber on the human's turn) with the glyph chip of the seat whose turn it is, and under it « n–m », the knights home of « nous » and « eux », with tabular figures. Accessible name: « Infos de la partie : {the turn line}, nous n sur 8, eux m sur 8 ». It opens a Sheet titled « Infos de la partie » holding the InfoBar in its column form (turn, dealer, deal n of 3, the two team pills) and « Fermer ».
- Toast: in this mode it is centred over the felt (the board's centre), never over a strip or the bottom ring row.
- DOM and Tab order: the pill, Réglages, Avance rapide, the lit knights, the tapis, the 7 or tray chips, the action buttons, the hand.
