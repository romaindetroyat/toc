# Phase 1 Plan 37: Sur téléphone, « Compacter l’écran » en hauteur et la barre compacte en largeur

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 3 and 4, sixth of eight plans)

After 01-36 a phone in portrait still shows the portrait galet in the 01-24 stack (info 56, action zone 120, hand 136): about 228 px wide at 390 x 664. Romain's « Compacter l'écran » (2026-10-14) stands for the phone: a thinner info bar, the action buttons on the hand's row, a hand a little lower and shorter. The planche « iPhone · galet » of « Sur les écrans » shows the same: a thin top bar, the board, the prompt row, the hand row with the action button beside it. A phone held sideways still shows the side panel; it gets the same dock under the landscape galet.

Budget (computed; Chromium emulation, insets 0; board height = (1.5 + 0.0354) x width on the portrait plateau, (0.5 + 0.0217) x width on the landscape one): portrait chrome 44 + 56 + 92 = 192 px from 375 px wide, 44 + 56 + 48 + 92 = 240 below; width = min(100vw − 32 px − insets, (100dvh − chrome − insets) / 1.5354, 880 / 1.5): 390 x 664 ≈ 307, 375 x 667 ≈ 309, 390 x 844 and 390 x 763 → 358, 320 x 480 ≈ 156. The regular fan's box (card + 36 px) would be 126 px with 64 x 90 cards and cost about 22 px of board: in the dock the fan's box is exactly the 92 px hand row, cards clipped at the bottom. Compact bar 44 + 92 = 136 px: 480 x 320 ≈ 353, 844 x 390 ≈ 487.

<objective>
Give the phone a compact chrome whose rendered heights are exactly those the board formula subtracts, in portrait and held sideways, so the galet grows to its targets with every state of the turn still playable by thumb and keyboard.

Purpose: Romain's « Compacter l'écran » on the galet; the side panel replaced on phones held sideways (UAT tests 3 and 4).
Output: shared height properties, the dock in ActionZone, the 44 px info bar, the compact fan (64 x 90 in a 92 px box), the tray and the 7 assistant in the dock, the compact bar in landscape, e2e/compact.spec.ts with heights, targets and captures.
</objective>
