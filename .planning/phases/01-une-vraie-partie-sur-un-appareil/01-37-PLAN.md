# Phase 1 Plan 37: Sur le téléphone tenu en largeur, la barre compacte

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 3 and 4, sixth of ten plans)

Since Romain's « Oui, paysage obligatoire », the phone plays the landscape galet held sideways. After 01-36 such a screen (data-chrome compact-bar) still shows the board in the narrow side panel of 01-24. This plan gives it the compact bar, which is how « Compacter l'écran » applies to the phone held sideways.

The bar of 01-33's contract (a 44 px row over a 92 px hand row, 136 px) gives cells about 15 px apart on an installed iPhone 13 held sideways: (390 − 21 − 136) / 0.52166 ≈ 447 px, × 107.05 / 3200 ≈ 15 px. The target of the coordinator's brief is cells about 20 px apart. So this plan revises the derived items 8 to 10 (« à confirmer par Romain ») and builds the revised bar:
- The info moves into a 96 px column at the left of the board, where a height-limited board leaves room anyway.
- Under the board, one 64 px row holds the hand, the prompt and the buttons from 640 px wide.
- Below 640 px wide, two rows: 56 + 64 = 120 px.

Size budget (Chromium emulation, insets 0; the board with its head room is 0.52166 times as tall as wide; step = width × 107.05 / 3200):

| Window | Bar | Calculation | Board | Step |
|--------|-----|-------------|-------|------|
| 844 x 390 | 64 | min(844 − 108, (390 − 64) / 0.52166) | ≈ 625 | 20.9 |
| 750 x 342 (Playwright « iPhone 13 landscape ») | 64 | min(642, 278 / 0.52166) | ≈ 533 | 17.8 |
| 667 x 375 | 64 | min(559, 596) | ≈ 559 | 18.7 |
| 568 x 320 | 120 | min(460, 200 / 0.52166) | ≈ 383 | 12.8 |
| 480 x 320 | 120 | min(372, 383) | ≈ 372 | 12.4 |

Real devices (computed, not asserted; for Romain's re-test):
- iPhone 13 installed, held sideways (844 x 390, insets 47 + 47 at the sides, 21 at the bottom): min(844 − 94 − 108, (390 − 21 − 64) / 0.52166) ≈ 585 px, cells ≈ 19.6 px apart. That meets the target of about 20 px.
- In Safari held sideways the visible height depends on Safari's bars:
  - with 342 px (Playwright's figure): ≈ 533 px (17.8 px);
  - if Safari reports the 21 px bottom inset: ≈ 493 px (16.5 px).

Width check of the 64 px row at 640 px:
- 640 − 24 margins = 616.
- The hand of five 64 px cards with 44 px slices is 240.
- The buttons' area is 184 (two buttons of about 88 px with a gap).
- With two 8 px gaps, that leaves a prompt of 176 px: three lines of Label 14, for example a preview's move and capture lines.
