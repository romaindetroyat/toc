# Phase 1 Plan 42: Règle G16, le cheval qui peut rentrer ne dépasse pas sa maison (moteur)

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 26, gap G-01-26)

Romain, recette 3 du 2026-10-15 : « J'ai l'impression que l'entrée dans la zone d'arrivée ne fonctionne pas toujours : si l'on peut atteindre une case vide de la zone d'arrivée, il faut que ce soit le seul coup à jouer ». À « que doit-on interdire ? », il a répondu « Seulement pour ce cheval ».

Diagnostic (planner, 300 self-play games, level-1 bots, recorded in the G-01-26 root_cause of 01-UAT.md): the engine has no bug. When the count of the card played reaches an empty, unobstructed home slot, the knight always enters. But a knight often passes its home because:
- the count overshoots (70 %);
- the target slot is occupied (28 %);
- a home knight is in the way (2 %, G2).
And 16 % of the passes were knights that another card of the same hand (1415 cases), or another split of the 7 (285 cases), would have brought home. That is what G16 forbids.

G16, the planner's reading, « à confirmer par Romain » (01-CONTEXT.md, « Recette 3 »): a knight that a card of the hand can bring into an empty home slot may not pass its entry with any card or any step of the 7. The player may still play another knight or another card. The 4 and the Valet are not concerned.

<objective>
Implement G16 in the pure engine, switchable like G14 and G15, behind the unchanged public API, with its French reason copy.

Purpose: a knight that can go home never goes round again by the player's (or a bot's) choice.
Output:
- KNIGHT_MUST_ENTER;
- entryDuty;
- the G16-aware legal list, playability and 7 search;
- the reason « must-enter »;
- entry.test.ts, flags and property tests.
</objective>
