# Phase 1 Plan 26: Le 7 dépasse le partenaire sans le manger (G15)

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 7)

Romain, during the acceptance test (01-UAT.md test 7, verbatim): « Le 7 ne doit pas manger les partenaires quand il les double, mais seulement s'il arrive sur eux (pas le choix). »

Root cause (verified): ringStretch() in packages/engine/src/path.ts captures every passed knight that is not « own » (capturePassed and not own), so the partner's knight is eaten when the 7 overtakes it (Q23, RULES.md R-10, scenario R-10.6). A planning probe over the fixtures found two scenarios that rely on it: R-10.6 (by design) and R-10.7 (its second victim, rouge0, is bleu's partner).

Target rule (G15): the 7 eats only OPPONENT knights it passes; it passes partner knights without eating them; landing on a partner knight still eats it (mandatory, as with any card, R-11); it still passes its own knights without eating, may not stop on one of its own knights, may not cross a protected knight (R-12). A player who has finished and plays his partner's knights (R-15, G7, G12): no team knight is ever eaten in passing.

The web app needs no code change: turnUi.ts builds the 7 preview from previewPlan() events and the move preview from resolveMove(); director.ts, cues.ts and Overlay.svelte read engine events only (checked while planning). A unit test proves the preview follows the engine.

<objective>
Make the 7 pass the partner's knights without eating them while still eating opponents it passes and any knight it lands on (Romain's decision of 2026-10-14, recorded as G15).

Purpose: close UAT gap « Le 7 dépasse les chevaux du partenaire sans les manger » (test 7, major).
Output: the RuleSet switch SEVEN_EATS_PASSED_PARTNER, the passing rule in ringStretch(), fixtures R-10.6 (inverted), R-10.7 (opponent), R-10.14 and R-15.10 with pictures, engine, flags, property and turnUi tests, and RULES.md, REQUIREMENTS.md (RULE-11), PROJECT.md and CONTEXT.md updated.
</objective>
