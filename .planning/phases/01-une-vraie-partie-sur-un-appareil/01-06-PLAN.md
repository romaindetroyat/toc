# Phase 1 Plan 06: Choisir sa couleur — écran de lancement, réglages mémorisés, polices et icônes

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** faire une partie complète de TOC contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés, **so that** je retrouve une vraie partie de TOC fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, avec l'ambiance du plateau original.

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan a player chooses his colour on the launch screen (remembered on this device), sees who his partner and opponents are, and the app wears its walnut and oak identity with self-hosted fonts and icons. Plan 01-08 then turns the board toward that colour and draws the sixteen knights. Generated outputs of tools/copy-vendor-assets.mjs (the six font and licence files) are listed as one `files_modified` entry, the same convention as docs/regles/R-*.webp.

<objective>
Deliver the launch screen with colour choice and persisted settings (D-06), and the self-hosted fonts and icons with the UI-SPEC design tokens.

Purpose: the table must feel like Romain's physical game from the first screen; settings and tokens are inputs to every later screen.
Output: settings module + store, Launch screen with ColorTile and SeatChip, vendor copy script, fonts, icons, tokens.
</objective>
