apps/web/src/game/turnUi.ts, the pure reducer of the turn interaction:
- selectCard(card, ctx): a 7 goes to buildSeven(card, [], undefined, ctx); a Valet goes to jackPick; otherwise candidatesOf(ctx, card) (exit/move actions of that card from ctx.legal). An exit plus advances gives modeChoice; anything else gives cardSelected { card, candidates }.
- previewState(card, candidates, action, ctx) gives the preview state (resolveMove of the engine).
- chooseMode(state, "exit" | "advance", ctx) leaves modeChoice.
- back(state, ctx) is the one-step-back of Échap: a preview goes back to cardSelected (or to modeChoice for an exit), and cardSelected goes back to modeChoice or IDLE.
- deselect(state) serves cancel, changeCard and escape.
- reduceTurnUi returns { state, submit?, hint? }; submit only for confirm, give and pose.
- The 7 state sevenBuilding has { plan, remaining, steps, preview, selected?, notice? }; confirmSeven submits when remaining is 0 and isComplete.

apps/web/src/ui/ActionZone.svelte, line ≈ 135: the cardSelected prompt picks one of two game.turn keys depending on `ui.candidates.length === 1`. The single-knight key and its sentence exist only for that branch.

apps/web/src/game/shortcuts.ts maps digits (cards), letters (lit knights), Entrée (confirm in a preview), Échap (back); it emits reducer events only.

Specs that assert the single-knight prompt: e2e/tracer.spec.ts (≈ line 199) and e2e/cartes-speciales.spec.ts (≈ line 75). e2e/clavier.spec.ts plays turns with digits, letters and Entrée.
