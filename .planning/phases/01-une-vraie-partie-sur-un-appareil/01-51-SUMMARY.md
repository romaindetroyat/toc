# Phase 1 Plan 51: La page d'essai des sons à choix, et les sabots en quatre versions Summary

**Every sound is now a list of versions with a chosen one. On #/sons Romain hears each version, picks one per sound (« Mon choix »), and the pick is saved on his device and heard at once in his games. « Copier mes choix » copies « Sabots : B »… to send. The hooves have four versions, all generated by the code, each with three takes: A (today), B « sur la terre », C « sur les pavés », D « sur le bois ». The old ratings are gone.**

## What was built

### Task 1 (tracer), 7399794: versions, choices, the engine by version, the page, the e2e tracer

- `sounds.ts`: `VariantKey` « a »–« d », `VariantSource` (`synth` with recipe and takes, `file` with files and its LICENSES.md row id, `silence`), `SoundDef = { bus, variants }`. Every sound's « a » is today's recipe (the sabot: `hoof`, 3 takes). The sabot gains « b » (`hoof-terre`). The `files` / `prefer` A/B mechanism of 01-09 is gone.
- `choices.ts`: `DEFAULT_CHOICES` (all « a », with the comment saying Romain's sent list is copied here), `parseChoices` (whitelist: known ids, each with one of its own keys), `loadPicks` / `loadChoices` / `saveChoices` under `toc.soundchoices.v1` (try/catch everywhere), `choicesAsText` (« Sabots : B », U+00A0 before the colon, page order).
- `engine.ts`: takes keyed by sound and version, a `pending/ready/failed` state per version, one shared promise per version. `renderAll()` prepares only the chosen versions. `setChoices()` keeps the previous version until the new one is ready. `play(id, version)` renders on demand and plays once. `scheduleClops(…, version)` plays a version's cadence. A file version tries .m4a then .mp3 (injectable decoder). The shell exports `setChoices` and `play`, reads `loadChoices(localStorage)` at unlock, logs `variant` on cues and every started buffer in `played`. `loadRecordings`, `recordings` and the old `availability(id)` are replaced by `availability(id, version)`.
- `synth.ts`: `hoof-terre`, and the shared helpers (`strikes`, `noiseHit`, `toneHit`, seeded `fillNoise`).
- `SoundTest.svelte`: the page « à choix » (data-driven, so C and D appeared with Task 2).
- `sons.spec.ts` tracer « sons : choisir la version B des sabots la fait entendre dans la partie »: B is played on the page (4 clops in « b »), picked, stored as `{ sabot: "b" }`. Then a game with a Dame move: every sabot buffer played is « b ». After a reload, B is still checked. Passes on both projects.

### Task 2, 8a92216: C and D, the full page, the end of the ratings

- `hoof-paves`: a narrow 2.2–2.6 kHz click (Q 2, 50 ms), a 5 kHz tick, the iron shoe's ring (3.1 kHz and an inharmonic 4.4 kHz partial), a short 150→80 Hz thump. `hoof-bois`: two Q 6 resonances at 450 Hz and 1.1 kHz (90 and 60 ms), the plank's own tones, a faint click, no thump; each take is another plank (−5 % / +6 %). Like B, both strike twice (heel, then toe 18–50 ms later) with their own seed per take, so the takes differ in grain and rhythm as well as pitch.
- Measured in Chromium (brightness estimate, Hz, takes 0/1/2): A 604/653/702; B 293/301/349; C 2167/2304/2260; D 623/613/693. All peaks are normalised to 0.85, and every version lasts 0.20–0.24 s. D is about as bright as A, but it is a pitched, hollow, double knock with no thump, against A's single noisy clop.

## For Romain: how to use the page

Open `#/sons` (Réglages → « Tester les sons »). Touch the page once to start the sound.
- **Sabots row:** touch « A » to « D » to hear each version, as the trot of a 4-case move.
- **Your pick:** choose it in « Mon choix » (A–D). It is saved on this device at once (`toc.soundchoices.v1`), and your games on this device play it from the next move.
- **Your pick at other speeds:** « Cadence de ton choix » (1, 4, 7, 13 cases) and the distance slider play the chosen version.
- **Sending:** « Copier mes choix » copies one line per sound (« Sabots : C ») to send to the developer. Copied into `DEFAULT_CHOICES`, it becomes everyone's sound.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The game's clops do not go through `cue`**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the plan's tracer reads the sabot entries of `__tocDebug.audio.cues`. But the director sends the clops through `scheduleClops`, which never wrote the cue log. Adding clops to that log would have changed the exact cue lists and counts that animations.spec asserts.
- **Fix:** a new debug log, `played`, fed by the engine's `onPlay` hook: every buffer actually started, with its version and take. The tracer checks that every sabot entry is « b ». The cue log still gains `variant`, as planned.
- **Files modified:** apps/web/src/audio/engine.ts, apps/web/src/debug.ts
- **Commit:** 7399794

**2. [Rule 2 - Correctness] A failed chosen version falls back to « a »**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** without a fallback, a recorded version that cannot be decoded (Chromium has no AAC, a missing file) would leave its sound silent in the game.
- **Fix:** `activate()` prepares « a » when the chosen version fails and nothing plays yet. Unit-tested.
- **Commit:** 7399794

**3. [Scope] The page was written whole in Task 1**
- The page is driven by the catalogue, so writing it once in Task 1 made C and D appear with Task 2's recipes. Task 2 then rewrote the page specs, as planned. Between the two commits, the old page specs of sons.spec.ts did not match the new page; Task 2 replaced them.
