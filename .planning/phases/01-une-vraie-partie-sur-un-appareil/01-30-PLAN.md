# Phase 1 Plan 30: Le plateau C+ dans la partie : un plateau 2:3 dessiné, chaque cavalier à sa place

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4 and test 3 note, second of eight plans)

01-29 put C+ in packages/board (scene.ts, art.ts) and wrote the contract (01-UI-SPEC.md « Addendum 2026-10-14 — Plateau C+ »). This plan makes the game draw C+ on a 2:3 board and stands every knight, every hop and every motion on it. Because the overlays use `anchorOf` too, they follow the knights from this plan on; their knight-relative offsets and sizes are tuned in 01-31, the felt's cards move in 01-32, the compact phone screen comes in 01-33.

Why the anchor keeps its signature (planning finding): `anchorOf(place, k)` already takes the seat k (bleu 0, vert 1, rouge 2, jaune 3). On a square board, turning the art by k quarter turns and giving each colour the side k seats further are the same thing; on the 2:3 board only the second is possible, and `sceneXY(place, k)` does it. Keeping k means the Director, the session, the animator adapter and their tests stay as they are.

Size note (computed from the formulas): this plan keeps the chrome of 01-24 (info bar 56, action zone 120 or 128, hand 136 or 160), so a height-limited 2:3 board is narrower than the former square board for now: at 390 x 664 (the iPhone 13 viewport as Playwright's Chromium emulates it, safe-area insets 0) (664 − 312) / 1.5466 ≈ 228 px wide, ring knights about 13 px. This is the intermediate state only: Romain decided « Compacter l'écran », and 01-33 brings the board to at least 300 px there; 01-36 asserts the targets. Landscape keeps this plan's layout (the board height is the screen height less the 16 px outer margin above and below; 1280 x 800: (800 − 32) / 1.5466 ≈ 497 px, knights about 29 px).

<objective>

Output: BoardArt.svelte, Board.svelte without raster, anchorOf on the scene, Pawn at the C+ sizes, the 2:3 layout, the specs that read the board's shape, the whole suite green.
</objective>
