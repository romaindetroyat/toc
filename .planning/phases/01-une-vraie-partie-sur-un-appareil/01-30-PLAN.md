# Phase 1 Plan 30: Le plateau C+ dans la partie : cases, cavaliers et repères

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4 and test 3 note, second of four plans)

01-29 put C+ in packages/board (scene.ts, art.ts) and wrote the contract (01-UI-SPEC.md « Addendum 2026-10-14 — Plateau C+ »). This plan makes the game draw C+ and puts every knight, every move and every marker on it, with the 2:3 layout and the fit checks of 01-24. The felt's cards (FeltTable, card flights, the dealer disc, the active seat's felt ring) still use their former positions until 01-31; nothing else is left on the old geometry after this plan.

Why the anchor keeps its signature (planning finding): `anchorOf(place, k)` already takes the seat k (bleu 0, vert 1, rouge 2, jaune 3). On a square board, turning the art by k quarter turns and giving each colour the side k seats further are the same thing; on the 2:3 board only the second is possible, and `sceneXY(place, k)` does it. Keeping k means the Director, the session, the animator adapter and their tests stay as they are.

Size note for the executor and for Romain's backstop (computed from the formulas, chrome unchanged: info bar 56, action zone 120 or 128, hand 136 or 160): on a height-limited screen the 2:3 board is narrower than the former square board. iPhone 13 in Safari (390 x 664): board 228 x 341 px, ring knights about 13 px wide, cells 14 px apart (the square board gave 14.5 px knights, 17 px cells). Installed app (about 390 x 763): 291 x 437 px, knights about 17 px (as before). Taller phones (430 x 932): knights about 23 px (+25 %). 1280 x 800: board 499 x 748 px, knights about 29 px (32 before). C+ improves the organisation (own camp centred at the bottom, partner at the top, numbered cells, no rule frames) more than the size; if Romain finds it too small, the lever is a more compact portrait chrome (01-UI-REVIEW.md Priority Fix 1), recorded under Deferred.
