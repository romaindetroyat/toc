# Phase 1 Plan 40: Un seul plateau dans le code, le galet en largeur, et les numéros des images en Caveat

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 3 and 4, ninth of ten plans)

01-32 built two galet plateaus, landscape and portrait, behind a layout parameter whose default moved to landscape in 01-34. Since Romain's « Pour la version iphone, oblige à jouer en format paysage, ça permettra d'utiliser le même plateau » and « Oui, paysage obligatoire », only the landscape galet is played. The portrait one, its tests and the parameter itself are dead weight.

This plan:
- leaves one board in the code, with nothing changing on screen or in the pictures;
- then fixes what 01-33's SUMMARY reported: the rule pictures' cell numbers came out in the fallback font (DejaVu Sans), not the game's Caveat.

Why the renderer changes rasteriser for the board's drawing (checked by the planner in the scratchpad on 2026-10-14):
- sharp's librsvg (2.63) ignores an @font-face data URI.
- sharp's FreeType does not read WOFF2. A fontconfig directory holding caveat-latin.woff2 is listed by the system's fc-list, yet sharp still falls back. With a TTF in a custom fontconfig file, sharp does follow the file.
- No TTF or OTF of Caveat exists in node_modules: @fontsource-variable/caveat 5.3.0 ships WOFF2 only, and @fontsource/caveat is not installed.
- No font converter is installed.
- The Chromium of the e2e (apps/web's @playwright/test 1.64.0, the container's /opt/pw-browsers build) renders the same SVG text in Caveat from the repo's WOFF2 through @font-face, with `document.fonts.check('700 64px "Caveat"')` true, and two renders were byte-identical, PNG and webp alike.
So only the four board drawings (one per k) go through Chromium. The pawn layers and the composition stay in sharp.
