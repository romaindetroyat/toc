# Phase 1 Plan 29: La géométrie du plateau C+ et les images des règles

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4, first of five plans)

UAT test 4: « La visibilité en version iphone n'est pas très bonne. On va changer le plateau de jeu pour avoir son propre camp centré en bas, le camp du partenaire centré en haut, les rappels de règles (petits cadres entre les camps) sont inutiles car impossibles à voir. » On 2026-10-14 Romain chose the board « C+ » (« On part sur le plateau C+ pour le moment »): the planche « C+ · Portrait avec les idées du 01 » of https://claude.ai/artifact/5Z4nttzgv3VvxfbifroU1A, whose source is `design/plateau-maquette.dc.html` (`renderVals()`, variant « portrait », `idees01` true).

The five plans of this gap, in order on one branch:
- **01-29 (this plan):** the C+ geometry and drawing as pure data in packages/board, the RULES.md pictures redrawn on it, and the decision recorded in the project documents and in the UI contract.
- **01-30:** the game draws C+ and puts the knights and every overlay on it (2:3 layout, fit at five screen sizes).
- **01-31:** the felt and the cards on C+.
- **01-32:** « Compacter l'écran » (Romain, 2026-10-14): a compact phone-portrait chrome (thinner info bar, the action buttons on the hand's row, a shorter hand) so the 2:3 board grows to at least 300 px wide on an iPhone 13 in Safari.
- **01-33:** the raster board leaves the build, licences, final gates and Romain's backstop.

Design choice (planning, Claude's discretion, reversible): the geometry is computed in the mock-up's own units with its numbers verbatim, then multiplied by `SCENE.scale = 2`. Scene units are therefore 2000 x 3000, chosen so that a ring knight (0.92 s = 116 units) keeps about the 110 x 190 footprint that every overlay, animation and card constant of plans 01-08 to 01-28 was tuned for; the app keeps those constants instead of rewriting each one.

Port the C+ board of the approved mock-up into packages/board as pure geometry and a declarative drawing, redraw the 112 rule pictures on it, and record Romain's decision where the project's contracts live.

Purpose: one source of truth for where every place, card and decoration of C+ sits, shared by the game (01-30, 01-31) and the documentation.
Output: scene.ts, art.ts and their tests, the new docs/regles pictures, the amended PROJECT.md, CLAUDE.md, REQUIREMENTS.md, CONTEXT.md and the UI-SPEC addendum « Plateau C+ ».
</objective>
