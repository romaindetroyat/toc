# Phase 1 Plan 25: Entrer dans la maison sans avoir fait le tour complet (G14)

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 6)

Romain, during the acceptance test (01-UAT.md test 6, verbatim): « si le cheval n'a pas fait le tour complet et qu'il arrive en situation de pouvoir rentrer (par 4 ou par valet) doit pouvoir rentrer. Corrige pour respecter cette règle ». He also confirmed that entry stays mandatory when the count is exact and the target home slot is free.

Root cause (verified): `walk()` in packages/engine/src/path.ts only opens the entry for a progress q with q >= 70 and (q − 70) mod 72 = 0 (the full lap, D-04 / Q3). A knight that backed with the 4 from its exit has a negative progress (−1 to −4) and passes its carré 16 (progress −2) without entering.

Target rule (G14): any FORWARD arrival on, or crossing of, one's own carré 16 opens the entry at the exact count onto an empty slot, whether or not the full lap was made. One still never enters by moving backward. A knight just out of the stable cannot reach its carré 16 without a lap, so in practice the change concerns knights that backed with the 4; knights placed by the Valet already take the progress of their new cell (G1) and are covered by a regression scenario.

After this plan, a knight that backed with the 4 enters its home at the exact count, everywhere the engine is used: legal moves, previews (« Entre dans la maison. »), the 7 splitter, the level-1 bots and apply.

## Scope Sanity Justification

- **Why one plan for G14 and another (01-26) for G15:** both rule changes touch rules.ts, path.ts and RULES.md, but they are independent decisions with their own fixtures, tests and pictures. Keeping them apart keeps each executor well inside its context budget (about 70 000 tokens here) and lets each gap close on its own evidence. 01-26 runs after this plan (shared files).
- **Task 1 has 6 files** because the tracer must stay green in one commit: the two fixtures whose expected result flips (R-02.3 and R-13.12) and the path unit test that asserted the old rule fail as soon as walk() changes, and RULES.md must keep listing the same scenario ids for rules-trace.
- **Pictures** under docs/regles are outputs of `node tools/render-scenarios.mjs`, regenerated in Task 3; they are listed in files_modified so the commit includes them.
