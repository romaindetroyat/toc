# Phase 1 Plan 05: Les sons — moteur audio, page de test pour Romain, licences

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** faire une partie complète de TOC contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés, **so that** je retrouve une vraie partie de TOC fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, avec l'ambiance du plateau original.

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan Romain can open #/sons on his phone and his computer, play every horse, card and ambience sound, rate each one and copy his notes; every sound, font, icon and the board art has a traced licence shown in « Crédits et licences ». Runs in parallel with Romain's rules validation (plan 01-03). The game wiring of these sounds to events is in plan 01-10; the settings sheet that exposes the mixes is in plan 01-11.

<objective>
Complete the audio engine (all synthesized sounds, buses, voice pool, jitter, ambience, recording loader), build the #/sons listening page for Romain (D-22), and create the licence manifest with its credits sheet (FX-05).

Purpose: D-22 asks Romain to validate the sounds by ear; doing it early, while the engine is being built, keeps audio off the critical path. Synthesis is the shipped baseline because no CC0 neigh could be confirmed from the development environment.
Output: audio modules + tests, SoundTest route, Toast/Sheet/Segmented components, LICENSES.md, licences parser, CreditsSheet, e2e spec.
</objective>
