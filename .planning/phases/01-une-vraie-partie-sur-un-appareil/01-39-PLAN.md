# Phase 1 Plan 39: Le plateau image et ses outils quittent le jeu ; la licence du plateau et les documents du projet

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4, eighth of ten plans)

After 01-32 to 01-38 the game draws the landscape galet on every screen and nothing in the app uses the raster board. This plan:
- removes the raster pipeline from the build: the compositing script, its generated images and the calibration tool of the quarters;
- updates the board's licence row to what ships;
- records Romain's decisions of 2026-10-14 in the project documents, quoting only his words: « galet partout », then « Oui, paysage obligatoire ».

The documents are 01-CONTEXT.md (which first records Romain's « Oui, mets-le à jour » about the instructions file), PROJECT.md, the one-line mirror in .claude/CLAUDE.md, REQUIREMENTS.md FX-01, the phase's success criterion 2 in ROADMAP.md (it still says « Sur un téléphone en portrait » and « le plateau original assemblé »), and the plan references of 01-CONTEXT.md. The quarters stay in assets/plateau as Romain's artwork.
