# Phase 1 Plan 35: Le tapis et ses cartes sur le galet en largeur

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4, fourth of ten plans)

After 01-34 the board, the knights and their markers stand on the landscape galet, and so do the seats' last cards. But the talon, the défausse, the dead hands, the « D » and the active seat's felt ring are still placed with the former 2560-unit board's constants (since the raster board). This plan lays them on the green ellipse of the landscape galet, as on the planche « Ordinateur · galet »: the seats' cards facing their seats, the talon and the défausse on either side of the centre.

<objective>
Lay every card and marker of the felt on the landscape galet and make every card motion start and land there.

Purpose: complete the galet table (D-08): who played what in front of each seat, as at the real table.
Output: cardFx.ts and its tests on the ellipse, FeltTable, FaceUpCard and CardFlights on the galet's card, the active seat ring and the debug view of the felt, two e2e checks.
</objective>
