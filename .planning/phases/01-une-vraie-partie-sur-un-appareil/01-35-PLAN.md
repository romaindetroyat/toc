# Phase 1 Plan 35: Le tapis et les cartes sur les deux plateaux galet

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4, fourth of eight plans)

After 01-34 the knights and their markers follow the plateau. The felt's cards still read the portrait plateau (the play flight and the seats' cards through the transitional default) or even the former 2560-unit board (the talon, the défausse, the dead hands and the « D », placed by the quarter-turn point rotation of the old layout). This plan lays every card of the felt on the galet felt of the plateau that is drawn: the green ellipse on the computer, the rounded rectangle on the phone, with the cards facing their seats as on the approved mock-up.

Mock-up-derived choice, « à confirmer par Romain » (reversible): the seats' cards face their seats (the mock-up's ROT) and the piles sit where the mock-up puts them; « Sur le tapis » stays the readable view on a phone.

<objective>
Lay every card of the felt on the galet felt of either plateau and make every card motion start and land there.

Purpose: complete the galet table (D-08): who played what in front of each seat, as at the real table.
Output: cardFx.ts on the plateau with its tests, FeltTable, FaceUpCard and CardFlights on the plateau's card, Board and Game passing the layout, anchorOf without its default, one e2e check on the landscape plateau.
</objective>
