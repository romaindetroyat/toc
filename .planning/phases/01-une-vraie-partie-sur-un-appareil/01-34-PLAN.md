# Phase 1 Plan 34: Les cavaliers, leurs halos, leurs aperçus, leurs marques et leurs cibles sur les deux plateaux galet

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4 and test 3 note, third of eight plans)

After 01-32 and 01-33 the board, its riders and the knights' anchors and hops follow the plateau, but the knights' sizes and every knight marker (halos, targets, ghosts, badges, letters, capture marks, the seat glow and ring, the party, the debug view) still read the portrait plateau through the transitional default. This plan gives them the layout, so a turn reads the same on the landscape plateau as on the portrait one. The landscape plateau is still reached with `plateau=largeur` (debug builds) until 01-36 lets the screen choose; the felt and its cards come in 01-35.

Output: Pawn, HitLayer, Overlay, Particles and DebugGeometry on the plateau's geometry; Board and Game passing the layout; anchor tests for both plateaus; e2e on the landscape plateau.
</objective>
