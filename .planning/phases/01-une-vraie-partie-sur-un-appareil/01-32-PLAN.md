# Phase 1 Plan 32: Compacter l’écran sur téléphone pour agrandir le plateau C+

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 3 and 4, fourth of five plans)

After 01-30 the 2:3 board fills the height left by the chrome of 01-24 (info bar 56, action zone 120, hand 136), which leaves it only about 228 px wide on an iPhone 13 in Safari. On 2026-10-14 Romain chose « Compacter l'écran »: a thinner info bar, the action buttons on the hand's row (no separate action-zone band on a phone in portrait), a hand a little lower and shorter, keeping the 44 px targets, the copy, the focus order, the keyboard hints, the 7 assistant and the tray. 01-29 wrote the contract in 01-UI-SPEC.md (« Écran compact (téléphone en portrait) ») and recorded the decision in 01-CONTEXT.md (D-07). This plan builds it and asserts the board size targets.

Budget behind the targets (planning computation): chrome = 44 + 148 px from 375 px wide (prompt row 56 + hand row 92) and 44 + 196 px below (a 48 px buttons row between them); board width = (screen height − chrome) / (1.5 + head room 0.0466), capped by the width less 32 px: 390 x 664 → about 305 px (305 x 458, ring knights about 18 px, cells about 19 px apart); 375 x 667 → about 307 px; 390 x 844 → 358 px (width-limited); 320 x 480 → about 155 px; landscape unchanged (480 x 320 → 112 px, 1280 x 800 → 499 px).

<objective>
Give the phone in portrait a compact chrome so the C+ board grows to at least 300 px wide on an iPhone 13 in Safari, with every state of the turn still playable by thumb and keyboard.

Purpose: Romain's « Compacter l'écran »: C+ readable on his iPhone (UAT tests 3 and 4).
Output: the dock in ActionZone (prompt row, hand row with the button column), the 44 px info bar, 64 x 90 cards, the tray and the 7 assistant in the dock, e2e/compact.spec.ts with the size targets, adapted fit specs, the whole suite green.
</objective>
