# Phase 1 Plan 32: Le plateau galet en largeur dans la vraie partie, un cavalier y bondit jusqu’à sa case ; la géométrie et le dessin des deux plateaux galet

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4 and test 3 note, first of eight plans)

Romain rejected board C+ once he saw it in the game (01-UAT.md, test 4, decision line: « trop laid », « trop plat, sans charme », « la forme », « on ne voit rien sur ordi », « Il faut une version iphone et une version ordinateur, mais avec des graphismes adaptés »). He chose the wooden « galet » board of design/plateau-galet.dc.html: « J'aime bien le galet » (after the felt transparency fix), confirmed « galet partout »: the computer in landscape (variant pc-galet), the iPhone in portrait (variant tel-galet).

The eight plans of this gap, one per wave (23 to 30), on one branch:
- **01-32 (this plan):** the two galet plateaus as pure data in packages/board; the tracer: the landscape galet in the real game at 1280 x 800, one knight hopping onto its cell.
- **01-33:** Romain's riders shipped and drawn, the RULES.md pictures on the galet, the licence row, the UI contract « Plateau galet » and the decision log.
- **01-34:** the knights, their halos, previews, letters, markers and hit targets on both plateaus.
- **01-35:** the felt and the cards on both plateaus.
- **01-36:** the screen chooses its plateau, live on rotation and resize; on a computer the landscape board across the width with the bottom bar.
- **01-37:** on a phone: « Compacter l'écran » in portrait and the compact bar in landscape.
- **01-38:** the raster board and its tools leave; licences; PROJECT.md, CLAUDE.md, REQUIREMENTS.md.
- **01-39:** the old geometry leaves packages/board, every gate, then Romain's re-test.

Staging (planning finding): every consumer of the board geometry must learn the layout, which touches about twenty files; with plans of at most 11 files, the geometry functions take the layout as a last parameter whose transitional default is the portrait galet. The portrait galet is 2:3 like C+, so every consumer and spec that does not pass a layout yet keeps working unchanged. The game draws the portrait galet by default until 01-36; the landscape galet is reached with the debug parameter `plateau=largeur` (debug builds only). 01-39 removes the defaults, so the typecheck proves that every caller passes its layout.

Scene units (design choice, reversible): the mock-up x 2 for both layouts, so the knights (0.95 step on the ring = about 102 units) keep about the 110 x 190 sprite footprint every overlay, card and motion constant was tuned for, and the portrait scene keeps the 2000 x 3000 viewBox of C+.
