# Phase 1 Plan 32: Le plateau image quitte le jeu ; licences, vérifications et l’avis de Romain

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4, last of four plans)

After 01-29 to 01-31 the game draws C+ and nothing in the app uses the raster board or its calibrated layout. This plan removes the raster pipeline from the build (the quarters stay in assets/ as Romain's artwork), removes the former layout modules and their calibration tool, updates the licence register, takes captures for Romain and runs every gate, then hands the board to Romain for the end-of-phase backstop on his iPhone and his computer.

Size note for Romain's review (from 01-30, chrome unchanged): in Safari on an iPhone 13 the 2:3 board is about 228 x 341 px and the ring knights about 13 px wide (the square board gave about 14.5 px); in the installed app about 291 x 437 px and 17 px (as before); at 1280 x 800 about 499 x 748 px and 29 px (32 before). If Romain finds C+ too small on the phone, the lever is a more compact portrait chrome (recorded under Deferred), not the board.

<objective>
Ship C+ alone: no raster board in the build or the bundle, no former layout in the code, the licence register up to date, every gate green, and Romain's verdict on his devices.

Purpose: finish UAT gap test 4 (and the size note of test 3) and leave a clean single source of board geometry.
Output: build scripts without compositing, the former layout and calibration removed, LICENSES.md row, captures, green gates, the human check.
</objective>
