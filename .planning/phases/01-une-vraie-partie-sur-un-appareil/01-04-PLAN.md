# Phase 1 Plan 04: Le moteur audio — sabots, hennissements, cartes et ambiance

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** faire une partie complète de TOC contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés, **so that** je retrouve une vraie partie de TOC fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, avec l'ambiance du plateau original.

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan the game can voice every Phase 1 moment: hooves on the audio clock with jitter and a voice limit, six neighs, three card sounds and a looping ambience, each mixed on its own bus, with recordings tried first and synthesis as the shipped baseline. Only the tracer (plan 01-01) is needed, so it runs in parallel with the board hardening and the rules. Plan 01-09 builds Romain's listening page on it; plan 01-19 wires these sounds to game events; plan 01-23 exposes the mixes in « Réglages ».

<objective>
Complete the audio engine started by the tracer: the AudioBackend seam with a fake for tests, the SOUNDS catalogue, cue/jitter/voice pool, clops on the audio clock, three buses, the recording loader with synthesis fallback, and every synthesized sound including the loopable ambience (D-22, D-23, D-25).

Purpose: FX-04 and FX-05 rely on one audio engine whose timing is testable without a browser; synthesis is the shipped baseline because no CC0 neigh could be confirmed from the development environment.
Output: apps/web/src/audio/{backend,engine,engine.test,synth,sounds}.ts.
</objective>
