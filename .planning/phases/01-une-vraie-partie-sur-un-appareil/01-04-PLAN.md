# Phase 1 Plan 04: La table — couleur, plateau tourné vers soi, cavaliers Staunton

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** faire une partie complète de TOC contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés, **so that** je retrouve une vraie partie de TOC fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, avec l'ambiance du plateau original.

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan a player chooses his colour (remembered on this device), sits at the table with his colour facing him, and sees the sixteen painted-wood knights in their stables. Runs in parallel with the RULES.md work (plan 01-02): it needs no rules, only the validated board topology.

<objective>
Deliver the launch screen with colour choice and persisted settings (D-06), the self-hosted fonts and icons with the UI-SPEC design tokens, the board rotation toward the player (D-05), and the four-colour Staunton knights with direction-aware facing (D-09, D-10, FX-02).

Purpose: the table must feel like Romain's physical game from the first screen; the knight art and the rotation are inputs to every later game and motion plan.
Output: settings module + store, Launch screen, tokens/fonts/icons, rotate and geometry modules, KnightDefs and Pawn components, rotated Board.
</objective>
