# Phase 1 Plan 31: Le tapis et les cartes sur C+

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 4, third of five plans)

After 01-30 the board, the knights and every knight marker are on C+, but the felt's cards (FeltTable, the card flights of cardFx.ts, the dealer disc and the active seat's felt ring) are still placed with the former 2560-unit constants. This plan puts them on the C+ felt of the mock-up: the vertical green rectangle in the middle, with the last card of each seat in front of it and facing it, the talon above the centre and the défausse below.

Design choice (reversible): the seats' cards face their seats as in the approved mock-up (the mock-up's ROT 0, 90, 180, 270), instead of staying upright as the original UI-SPEC wanted; a French card reads the same both ways up, and the « Sur le tapis » sheet stays the readable view on a phone (01-UI-SPEC.md addendum « Plateau C+ »).

<objective>
Lay every card of the felt on C+ and make every card motion start and land at the C+ places.

Purpose: complete the C+ table (D-08 revised): the felt shows who played what in front of each seat, as at the real table.
Output: cardFx.ts, FeltTable.svelte, FaceUpCard.svelte, CardBack.svelte and the active seat ring of Overlay.svelte on the scene; cardFx tests; one e2e check; the whole suite green.
</objective>
