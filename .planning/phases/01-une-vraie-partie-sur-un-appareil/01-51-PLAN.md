# Phase 1 Plan 51: La page d'essai des sons à choix, et les sabots en quatre versions

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT test 29, gap G-01-29; first of three sound plans)

Romain, recette 3 du 2026-10-15 : « les sons ne sont pas bons, il faut les retravailler ». Son choix : « Page d'essai à choix (Recommandé) ».

Planner's reading, « à confirmer par Romain » (01-CONTEXT.md, « Recette 3 »):
- three or four versions of each sound on #/sons;
- a choice per sound, heard at once in the game on his device;
- « Copier mes choix » to make them everyone's choice;
- today's sounds stay until he picks.
Every version is generated by the code, or CC0 / public domain with its own licence row (CLAUDE.md).

This plan builds the mechanism end to end and gives the hooves their four versions. 01-52 does the neighs, 01-53 the cards and the new end-of-game sounds.

<objective>
Turn each sound into a list of versions with a chosen one, rebuild #/sons as a page to listen and choose, and give the hooves four generated versions.

Purpose: Romain chooses his sounds by ear, as he asked (D-22 and D-23 as revised on 2026-10-15: generated by the code or CC0, licence traced, validated by ear on a test page).
Output:
- sounds.ts versions;
- choices.ts;
- the engine plays and renders by version;
- SoundTest.svelte « à choix »;
- the hoof recipes B–D;
- the ratings removed;
- specs and contract.
</objective>
