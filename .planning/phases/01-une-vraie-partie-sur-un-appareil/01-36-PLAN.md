# Phase 1 Plan 36: L’écran choisit son plateau, en direct ; sur ordinateur, le plateau en largeur et la barre du bas

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 4 and 5, fifth of eight plans)

After 01-32 to 01-35 every layer of the board follows the plateau, but the game still draws the portrait galet unless a debug parameter asks for the other one, and a computer still shows it in the narrow side panel (« on ne voit rien sur ordi »). This plan lets the screen choose its plateau, live, and gives the computer the layout of the planche « Sur les écrans »: the board across the width and one bar under it with the info, the hand and the actions.

Screen modes (one store, the same media queries in the CSS; thresholds « à confirmer par Romain »):
- **bar**: landscape, at least 960 px wide and 600 px high (computers, tablets held sideways): built here.
- **compact-bar**: the other landscape screens (phones held sideways, small windows): today's side panel with the landscape plateau for one more plan; 01-37 brings the compact bar.
- **dock**: portrait below 600 px wide (phones): today's stack for one more plan; 01-37 brings « Compacter l'écran ».
- **stack**: portrait from 600 px wide: today's stack, unchanged.

Size budget (computed, Chromium emulation, insets 0; head room ≈ 0.0217 of the width on the landscape plateau, so the board is (0.5 + 0.0217) times as tall as wide with its head room): bar 160 px (the regular fan's box: an 88 x 124 card + 36 px). 1280 x 800 → min(1248, (800 − 160) / 0.5217, 1600) ≈ 1227 px (step ≈ 41 px, ring knights ≈ 39 px; the planche showed 1280 with no room for the top-row knights' heads); 1024 x 640 → min(992, 920) ≈ 920; 1440 x 900 → min(1408, 1418) = 1408; 1920 x 1080 → the 1600 px cap (the mock-up's own size). Width of the bar at 1024 px: info ≈ 240 + the fan of five regular cards ≈ 344 + action ≥ 320, with the gaps, fits.

Live switch (planning finding): the Director already finishes running motions through the animator (`finish`, `finishCards`) when it snaps; the game screen makes the same calls when the plateau changes, so the Director's promises settle, its queue goes on, and each next move reads the new layout at its start (01-32). No change to director.ts or session.svelte.ts.
