## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 3 and 4, last of eight plans)

After 01-29 to 01-35 the game draws C+ alone, on a compact phone screen. This plan records the board-width targets at every supported size, takes captures for Romain, runs every gate, and hands the phase to Romain's re-test in 01-UAT.md.

Size targets (01-UI-SPEC.md « Écran compact », computed from the formulas, asserted with a margin, all in Playwright's Chromium emulation with safe-area insets 0): portrait chrome 192 px from 375 px wide and 240 px below, board width = (height − chrome − insets) / 1.5466 capped by the width less the two 16 px gutters; landscape board height = the screen height less the 16 px outer margin above and below (32 px in all, none on a screen 400 px high or less), its width at most the screen width less 368 px (the 320 px panel, the 16 px gap and two 16 px outer margins), so width = min((height − 32 px − insets) / 1.5466, width − 368 px − insets). 390 x 664 ≈ 305 → ≥ 300 (knights +22 %, cells +13 % against the former square board); 375 x 667 ≈ 307 → ≥ 295; 390 x 844 → 358 → ≥ 350; 390 x 763 → 358 → ≥ 350; 320 x 480 ≈ 155 → ≥ 150; 480 x 320 → 112 → ≥ 105; 1280 x 800 ≈ 497 → ≥ 470.

Real devices (computed, for Romain's re-test, not asserted): iPhone 13 installed as a standalone app, 390 x 844 with insets 47 px top and 34 px bottom → (844 − 81 − 192) / 1.5466 ≈ 369, width-limited at 358 px: the target holds. iPhone 13 in Safari with its toolbars: insets assumed 0 → ≈ 305 px. If Safari reports the 34 px bottom inset while its toolbar is shown: (664 − 34 − 192) / 1.5466 ≈ 283 px, under the 300 px target; said here plainly, and settled by Romain on his device.

UAT traceability (01-UAT.md): test 1 and 2 (sounds) deferred by Romain; test 3 (knight size note) and test 4 (board) closed by 01-29 to 01-36 and re-tested by Romain; test 5 (keyboard) closed by 01-27 and 01-28; test 6 (entering the home without a full lap, G14) closed by 01-25; test 7 (the 7 passes the partner without eating him, G15) closed by 01-26.
