# Phase 1 Plan 36: Sur ordinateur, le galet en largeur et la barre du bas

## Phase Goal

Un joueur fait une partie complète de TOC, fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés.

## This Plan's Slice (gap closure, UAT tests 4 and 5, fifth of ten plans)

After 01-34 and 01-35 the landscape galet is the only board, with every layer on it, but a computer still shows it in the narrow side panel of 01-24 (912 px at 1280 x 800; Romain about C+: « on ne voit rien sur ordi »). This plan builds the computer layout of the planche « Sur les écrans » (item 7 of the addendum): the board across the width and one bar under it with the info, the hand and the actions. It also adds the screen store whose three modes the next plans fill: the compact band for phones held sideways (01-37) and « Tourne ton téléphone » for screens taller than wide (01-38).

Size budget (computed, Chromium emulation, insets 0; the board with its head room is (0.5 + 0.02166) times as tall as wide): bar 160 px (the regular fan's box: an 88 x 124 card + 36 px). 1280 x 800 → min(1248, (800 − 160) / 0.52166, 1600) ≈ 1227 px (step ≈ 41 px, ring knights ≈ 39 px); 1024 x 640 → min(992, 920) ≈ 920; 1440 x 900 → min(1408, 1418) = 1408; 1920 x 1080 → the 1600 px cap (the mock-up's own size). Width of the bar at 1024 px: info ≈ 240 + the fan of five regular cards ≈ 344 + action ≥ 320, with the gaps, fits.

<objective>
Give the computer the landscape galet across the width with the bottom bar, every state of the turn playable from it, and the screen store the phone plans build on.

Purpose: Romain's « galet partout » with the computer in landscape, keeping the keyboard play of UAT test 5.
Output: screen.svelte.ts, the bar layout in Game, the info bar's column form, the action zone and the 7 assistant in the bar, e2e/ecrans.spec.ts and finition.spec.ts with the computer targets.
</objective>
