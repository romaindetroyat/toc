# Phase 1 Plan 02: Le plateau, net et juste sur tous les écrans

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** faire une partie complète de TOC contre trois joueurs gérés par l'ordinateur, sur le plateau original, avec des cavaliers animés et sonorisés, **so that** je retrouve une vraie partie de TOC fidèle aux règles maison validées par Romain, avec l'ambiance du plateau original.

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan the board assembled by the tracer (plan 01-01) is sharp and uncropped from a 320 px phone to a desktop, shows a calm oak and felt backdrop while it loads and a working « Réessayer » when it fails, and has a debug overlay that proves every cell sits on Romain's drawing. Geometry drift, French typography and raw-HTML sinks are guarded by tests from now on. Runs in parallel with the engine contract (plan 01-03) and the audio engine (plan 01-04).

## Scope Sanity Justification

`files_modified` lists 10 entries, at the 10-file warning threshold and well below the 15-file blocker. The count is accepted rather than split, for these reasons:

1. **Half of the entries are test files with no production code.** Task 2 writes five independent guards (tools/calibrate-board/calibrate.test.ts, packages/engine/src/topology.test.ts, packages/board/src/layout.test.ts, apps/web/src/i18n/typography.test.ts, apps/web/src/no-raw-html.test.ts); none depends on another, and each checks code that plan 01-01 already wrote (the typography guard also covers Task 1's board copy).
2. **Task 1 is one board slice.** Board.svelte, Game.svelte, DebugGeometry.svelte, the French board copy and e2e/board.spec.ts deliver the srcset, loading, error and debug states of the same board.
3. **Context budget.** 40 000 tokens, ratio 0.40 of the 100 000 smart-zone budget (estimate-check); 2 tasks of 5 files each.
