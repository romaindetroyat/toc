# Phase 2 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 2 « Jouer en ligne entre proches ». Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Sources: `02-CONTEXT.md` (D-01..D-14, locked, including the addendum of 2026-10-17), `02-RESEARCH.md` (Patterns 5-14, Pitfalls 5, 7, 10), the phase-1 contract `01-UI-SPEC.md` with its addenda (galet, « Le plateau sur toute la hauteur du téléphone », « La planche de fin de partie », keyboard), the phase-3 contract `03-UI-SPEC.md` (strength picker, launch layouts, thinking sign, « Des cartes qu’on lit », « 1er » token), and the current code: `apps/web/src/App.svelte`, `routes/Launch.svelte`, `routes/Game.svelte`, `ui/StrengthPicker.svelte`, `ui/InfoBar.svelte`, `ui/EndScreen.svelte`, `ui/SettingsSheet.svelte`, `ui/Sheet.svelte`, `ui/ConfirmDialog.svelte`, `ui/Toast.svelte`, `ui/RotatePrompt.svelte`, `ui/icons.ts`, `app.css`, `i18n/fr/*.ts`, `packages/board/src/scene.ts` (`riderBox`, corners).
> This document is in English. Every user-facing string is French and quoted as it must appear. Copy is written here with plain spaces before `! ? : ;` and inside « »; the catalogue applies the phase-1 typography (U+202F before `; ! ? »` and after `«`, U+00A0 before `:`, typographic apostrophe `’`, U+202F between a number and its unit). `i18n/typography.test.ts` must cover every new catalogue file.
> No question was asked of Romain for this contract (orchestrator instruction): items marked **(à confirmer par Romain)** are researcher choices inside D-01..D-14. They are collected in French in the last section and do not block planning.
> Gender-neutral French is a rule of this phase: player names are unknown first names, so no participle or adjective agrees with a player (« a rejoint la table », « est de retour », « en ligne », never « connecté », « invité », « parti »).

**Scope (online play only; the local game against the computer is unchanged, D-09):**
1. Home « Accueil » with « Jouer contre l’ordinateur » / « Créer une table en ligne » and « Mes parties en cours » (D-06, D-09).
2. First-name prompt (D-01).
3. Table creation and the invite sheet: link, copy, QR, share (D-02).
4. Waiting room: seats by colour, take/leave a seat, the creator moves people and sets the computer strength, start (D-03).
5. In-game additions: connection state per player, own reconnecting banner, pause plate « En attente de X… » with countdown, the creator's three choices after 1 min, creator role handover, stopped/saved game, reclaim of one's own seat (D-04, D-05, D-06, D-12).
6. Quick-message chat: button, palette of the D-13 messages and emojis, received bubbles that do not hide the board (D-07, D-13).
7. End plank additions: « Revanche » with everyone's agreement state (D-08).
8. Errors: table full, table not found or expired, game already started, opened elsewhere, new version, no network.

Target sizes: 844 × 390, 750 × 342, 480 × 320 held sideways and 1280 × 800 on a computer must all fit (also checked: 667 × 375, 568 × 320, 640 × 360, 1024 × 640). On the waiting room and the game screen, portrait shows the existing « Tourne ton téléphone » (`RotatePrompt.svelte`, unchanged). The home, the first-name prompt and the error screens work in both orientations, like today's launch screen (reason: a link opened from WhatsApp usually arrives in portrait, and typing a first name with the keyboard up is easier upright).

- Tutoiement, short sentences, no bare « Annuler » anywhere, computer players named after famous horses (Tornado … Rossinante).

## Color

Unchanged 60 / 30 / 10 split; **no new colour token**.

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | Noyer sombre `#2B1D12` (`--noyer`) | Home, waiting room, error and prompt screens background; sticky band; plate veil (30 %) |
| Secondary (30%) | Chêne `#D8C6B0` (`--chene`) and Bois sombre `#4A2F1A` (`--bois-sombre`) | Oak: the two home choice tiles and the seat tiles (the only light surfaces of their screens). Bois sombre: « Mes parties en cours » rows, plates, sheets, the reconnect banner, rider badges |
| Accent (10%) | Ambre `#FFB02E` (`--ambre`) | Reserved list below |
| Destructive | Brique `#A32018` (`--brique`) | Not used in this phase (no destructive action is added: stopping saves, leaving pauses) |

Accent reserved for (phase-1 list applied to this phase's surfaces; nothing else):
1. One primary button per screen state: « Continuer » (first-name prompt), « Partager le lien » (invite sheet, or « Copier le lien » when the device cannot share), « Lancer la partie » (waiting room, creator only), « Confier la place » (level step of the pause plate), « Reprendre la partie » (saved plate, creator only), « Réessayer maintenant » (connection plate), « Revanche » (end plank), the primary of each error screen and the safe answer of each confirmation. The home has **no** amber: its two choices are oak tiles.
2. The outline (3 px) and check of **your own** seat tile in the waiting room (the existing « selected colour tile » style).
3. Keyboard focus ring (3 px amber, 2 px walnut offset) on every new control.
4. The own-turn dot (existing; never on a bot or a handed-over seat).

Never amber in this phase: chat bubbles, the message chips, the countdown, the crown, rider badges, connection states, the vote discs, the QR code.

Contrast checks (computed, WCAG relative luminance): walnut `#2B1D12` on oak `#D8C6B0` ≈ 9.8:1 (tile text; at 80 % opacity for the sub lines still > 7:1); cream on bois sombre ≈ 11.6:1, `--creme-attenuee` on bois sombre ≈ 6.4:1 (statuses); walnut on cream ≈ 15:1 (bubbles, link field, QR modules). QR modules are `--noyer` on `--creme` with the cream quiet zone (dark-on-light, as scanners expect). Colour is never the only cue: seat chips carry glyph and name, connection states carry an icon and a word, the bubble carries the sender's name.
