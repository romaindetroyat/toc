# Phase 2 Plan 07: Recharger, perdre le réseau, arrêter et reprendre des jours après

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** jouer la même partie de TOC avec trois autres, chacun sur son appareil, sur une table dont le serveur fait autorité, **so that** on joue ensemble à distance, sans triche possible, et qu'une coupure ne fasse jamais perdre la partie.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal; see 02-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan a reload or a lost network brings the player back to the exact game; Romain can stop the game whenever he likes and the family picks it up days later through the same link or « Mes parties en cours », exactly where it stood; a table forgotten for 30 days cleans itself up; and someone who began in WhatsApp's browser can get their own seat back with Romain's confirmation.

<objective>
Make the welcome snapshot the guaranteed resync (reload, network loss, second tab, protocol version), add the creator's stop at any time and the online leave, the 30-day expiry by alarm, and the host-approved reclaim of one's own seat; show the reconnect banner and plate, the saved state in « Mes parties en cours », the notices and the reclaim dialog.

Purpose: NET-02, HIST-01 and SC4 as Romain chose them (D-06, D-12).
Output: core and workerd resume tests, connection states in the client, the online « Réglages », ReclaimDialog, reprise.spec.ts.
</objective>
