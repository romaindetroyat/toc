# Phase 02 Plan 04b: Romain's logo as app icon and on the home Summary

The image Romain sent on 2026-10-17 (« Utilise cette image comme logo ») is now the favicon, the Apple touch icon and the PWA manifest icon (with a maskable variant), all generated by `tools/build-icons.mjs` with the existing sharp dev dependency. It also appears as a small rounded logo before « TOC » on the home in all four layouts, without changing how the home fits on screen.

## What was built

- **Source and generator.** The original is kept unchanged at `assets/logo/logo-source.webp` (1254 × 1254). Running `node tools/build-icons.mjs` produces the same bytes every time, so a re-run leaves nothing to commit. File sizes: icon-32 1.9 kB, icon-48 3.1 kB, apple-touch-icon 20.9 kB, icon-192 23.1 kB, icon-512 105.8 kB, maskable-512 53.1 kB, favicon.ico 6.1 kB, logo-128.webp 8.3 kB. The PNGs use a colour palette to stay small.
- **Links.** `index.html` gains `<link rel="icon">` for `/favicon.ico` and `/icons/icon-32.png`, `<link rel="apple-touch-icon">` and `<link rel="manifest">`. The manifest declares name and short_name « TOC », lang fr, start_url and scope `/`, display standalone, colours #2B1D12, and the 192 and 512 icons plus the maskable 512. There is no service worker.
- **Home.** `Home.svelte` imports the logo (Vite gives it a hashed name under `/assets/`) and places it before the title: 34 × 34 in the two-column and one-column layouts, 64 × 64 beside the title and tagline on the computer and in portrait, with 22 % rounded corners. Screenshots were checked at 844 × 390, 750 × 342, 480 × 320 and 1280 × 800.
- **Licences.** New row `logo` in `assets/audio/LICENSES.md` with the source « Logo fourni par Romain, 2026-10-17 », author Romain and « Utilisé avec l’accord de l’auteur ». It covers the source file, `apps/web/src/assets/logo/`, `apps/web/public/icons/` and `apps/web/public/favicon.ico`. The « Crédits et licences » sheet gets a « Logo » group with the name « Logo et icônes de l’application ». The coverage test now also scans `public/icons` and `public/*.ico`, and two tests were added: the icons are bundled, and the logo row exists.
- **Embedded deploy.** No change was needed: `wrangler.embarque.jsonc` already had Data rules for png, webp and ico and a Text rule for webmanifest, and `worker-embedded/index.js` already set their content types. After the web build, the embedded worker build bundles 38 files, the `wrangler deploy --dry-run` succeeds, and `tools/check-bundle.mjs` passes (« bundle OK: 38 separate modules »). The embedded Worker was also run locally with `wrangler dev` (then stopped): favicon.ico returned 200 image/x-icon, the PNGs 200 image/png, the manifest 200 application/manifest+json, and the logo 200 image/webp.
