# Phase 2 Plan 05: La salle d'attente et l'invitation

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** jouer la même partie de TOC avec trois autres, chacun sur son appareil, sur une table dont le serveur fait autorité, **so that** on joue ensemble à distance, sans triche possible, et qu'une coupure ne fasse jamais perdre la partie.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal; see 02-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan Romain creates a table, sends the link by WhatsApp or shows the QR code, sees his relatives arrive in the waiting room, lets each take a colour (or moves them), sets the strength of each computer seat and starts. The tracer's four seat buttons become the waiting room of 02-UI-SPEC.

Purpose: D-02 and D-03 as Romain chose them, D-12 enforced by the server.
Output: lobby core and tests, WaitingRoom, SeatTile, MoveSheet, Notice, InviteSheet, QrCode, two online specs.
</objective>
