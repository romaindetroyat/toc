# Phase 5 Plan 05: Les chiffres, le classement entre amis et le rapport des parties solo, côté serveur

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur de la famille, habitué ou nouveau venu, **I want to** retrouver mes parties, mes chiffres et le classement entre amis, apprendre les règles en jouant et reconnaître chaque place sans dépendre des couleurs, **so that** je me mesure à mes proches et que toute la famille puisse jouer.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 5 Goal; see 05-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

05-01 records who played and what each seat did. This plan answers the two other questions of SC2: « how am I doing, and with whom? » (« Mes chiffres ») and « where do I stand among my friends? » (« Classement »), and opens the door for the solo games the browser reports (D-01), which count for the player alone. 05-07 sends the solo reports; 05-09 draws the two panels.

Add the stats and ranking reads (pure aggregation in TypeScript over the 0003 rows, exact ordering, shared ranks) and the client-claimed solo report (validated, plausible, rate-limited, idempotent, never ranked), with workerd tests for every rule and refusal.

Purpose: SC2 (HIST-03, HIST-04) and the server half of D-01's solo games.
Output: aggregate.ts, three routes, three schemas, aggregate.test.ts, results-api.test.ts.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → POST /api/me/results/solo | Untrusted body; the game happened only in the browser |
| browser → GET stats, ranking | Session cookie; the user id comes from the session only |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-11 | Tampering | forged solo wins | medium | mitigate | never ranked (ranked 0, D-01, D-02); plausibility checks; 30 a day per account through PlayerHub.allow |
| T-05-12 | Tampering | replay or double submit of a report | low | mitigate | id solo:<user>:<uuid>, ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, the seat row bound to the played row's ended_at |

| T-05-15 | Information disclosure | friend enumeration through the ranking | low | mitigate | only the friendships of the session's user; no lookup by name or id |
| T-05-16 | Denial of service | flooding D1 with reports | medium | mitigate | 8192-byte body cap, 30 a day per account, one batch of two rows per report |
| T-05-17 | Tampering | two accounts of one person to farm the ranking | low | accept | family scale (research threat patterns); the ranking is shown among friends only |
| T-05-SC | Tampering | npm installs | high | mitigate | No package is added by this plan |
