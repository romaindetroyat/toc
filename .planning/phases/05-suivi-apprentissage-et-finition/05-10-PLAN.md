# Phase 5 Plan 10: L’audit d’accessibilité de chaque écran, et ses corrections

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur de la famille, habitué ou nouveau venu, **I want to** retrouver mes parties, mes chiffres et le classement entre amis, apprendre les règles en jouant et reconnaître chaque place sans dépendre des couleurs, **so that** je me mesure à mes proches et que toute la famille puisse jouer.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 5 Goal; see 05-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

SC5 asks that the whole interface be French, that each seat be recognisable other than by its colour (05-02 drew the marks) and that the touch targets be big enough. This plan measures every screen at four sizes with axe and the project's own 44 px rule, names every knight for a screen reader, checks the contrast of the tokens and the French of the catalogues, and fixes what it finds. VoiceOver on Romain's iPhone stays a UAT test (05-11).

<objective>
Install the approved audit tool, audit every route and sheet at four sizes (axe, 44 px, names, French, structure), test the token contrast and the catalogues' French, name the knights fully, fix every finding, and extend the keyboard spec to the phase's new screens.

Purpose: SC5 and APP-05 proven by tests that stay.
Output: the pinned package, audit.ts, two accessibilite specs, contrast.test.ts, denyList.test.ts, knightName.ts, the fixes, the extended clavier.spec.ts.
</objective>
