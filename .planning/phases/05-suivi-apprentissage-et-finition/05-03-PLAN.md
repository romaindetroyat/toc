# Phase 5 Plan 03: Le moteur des leçons — huit situations préparées, rejouées en test

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur de la famille, habitué ou nouveau venu, **I want to** retrouver mes parties, mes chiffres et le classement entre amis, apprendre les règles en jouant et reconnaître chaque place sans dépendre des couleurs, **so that** je me mesure à mes proches et que toute la famille puisse jouer.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 5 Goal; see 05-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

The tutorial (APP-04) is the game screen fed with prepared positions and a guide that checks each tap (05-RESEARCH Pattern 5: no second UI). This plan writes the eight lessons and their checking as pure code over the real engine and turn reducer, and proves in Node that each lesson can be played to its « Bravo » and that each wrong choice is explained and changes nothing. 05-06 puts them on screen; 05-08 adds the lessons page and the first-launch offer.

<objective>
Write the eight lessons as data (positions in RULES.md cells, hands, the others' scripted gives, steps with halos and lines), the pure lesson gate and step logic placed in front of the turn reducer, and createGame's `script` option, with a Node replay of every lesson and every wrong choice.

Purpose: SC4 « Un nouveau joueur apprend les règles en faisant une partie guidée », made safe before any screen: a script the engine refuses fails here, not in front of Romain (Pitfall 3).
Output: lessons.ts, lessonRunner.ts, their tests, the script option, lecons.ts.
</objective>
