# Phase 4 Plan 08: L’application installable — service worker, écran de démarrage, mise à jour sans recharger une partie, hors ligne

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** avoir mon compte, retrouver mes amis et lancer une table ou une partie solo depuis l'application installée, **so that** je joue avec eux sans recopier de lien et je suis prévenu quand c'est mon tour ou qu'on m'invite.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal; see 04-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan, Romain's mother adds TOC to her iPhone's home screen: it opens full screen on the walnut splash with the logo, works without network for a game against the computer, shows the notifications the server sends, and when a new version comes out she is asked to update on the home, never in the middle of a game.

<objective>
Build the service worker (precache, prompt update, push display), the update flow and its rules, the manifest and iOS splash images, the install guidance (strip, sheet, « Réglages »), the offline strip, and the test harness: service workers blocked in the existing suites, one PWA spec that allows them.

Purpose: SC4 (APP-01, APP-02) and the offline solo game from the installed app (ACCT-05); the display half of APP-03.
Output: sw.ts, pwa/*.ts, HomeStrip, InstallSheet, the manifest, the splash images, playwright.pwa.config.ts and pwa.spec.ts.
</objective>
