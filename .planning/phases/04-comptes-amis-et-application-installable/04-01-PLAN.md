# Phase 4 Plan 01: Traceur — les six outils installés, et le premier compte de bout en bout

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** avoir mon compte, retrouver mes amis et lancer une table ou une partie solo depuis l'application installée, **so that** je joue avec eux sans recopier de lien et je suis prévenu quand c'est mon tour ou qu'on m'invite.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal and its five success criteria. The ROADMAP `**Goal**` line is not written in user-story form; run `/gsd-mvp-phase 4` to formalise it if wanted.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan a proche opens an invitation link, chooses a pseudo, creates a passkey, keeps the backup code and lands on the home signed in, and stays signed in after a reload: the D1 schema, the shared rules, the Worker's sign-up and session, the browser's passkey ceremony, a route and the home tile, in one slice. The next plans deepen it, one wave each, in the order Romain's brief gives: 04-02 signing in on another device, signing out, passkeys and a new backup code; 04-03 the PlayerHub Durable Object (migration v2) and the recovery with the backup code; 04-04 the account at the table (sockets); 04-05 friends and invitations; 04-06 presence, « Inviter un ami » and the in-app notices; 04-07 « C’est ton tour » and Web Push; 04-08 the installable app; 04-09 to 04-11 the screens; 04-12 the full checks, the deploy steps for the orchestrator and Romain's tests on real phones.
