# Phase 4 Plan 05: Les amis — liens d’invitation (une fois, 7 jours), codes amis et la liste

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** avoir mon compte, retrouver mes amis et lancer une table ou une partie solo depuis l'application installée, **so that** je joue avec eux sans recopier de lien et je suis prévenu quand c'est mon tour ou qu'on m'invite.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal; see 04-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan, Romain sends his sister a link; she creates her account with it and is in his friends at once. His cousin already has an account: they exchange their friend codes. Everyone has a friends list and can remove someone. The server and the browser's account client do it here; « Mes amis » and the code sheet are drawn in 04-10.

<objective>
Add invitation links (create, list, disable, state, accept), friend codes (create, redeem with the hub's limit), the friends list and removal, and the device's memory of its own links.

Output: social.ts, the extended invitation state, the workerd tests, the client calls, account/invites.ts.
</objective>
