# Phase 4 Plan 04: Le compte à la table — la place de l’appareil passe au compte, et « Mes parties en cours » sur tous les appareils

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** avoir mon compte, retrouver mes amis et lancer une table ou une partie solo depuis l'application installée, **so that** je joue avec eux sans recopier de lien et je suis prévenu quand c'est mon tour ou qu'on m'invite.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal; see 04-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan, Léa, who played as a guest on her phone, creates her account on that phone and stays at her seat under her pseudo; she opens the same table on her computer, signed in, and takes her seat there (the phone is told « Partie ouverte ailleurs »). When she signs out of the family tablet, the tablet can no longer play her seat. Her account knows her tables on every device. Guests play exactly as in phase 2.

<objective>
Carry the account from the session cookie to the table: the Worker forwards it on the upgrade, the GameRoom stores it, the pure core resolves the person with the four rules of 04-RESEARCH Pattern 4 and links seats; D1 keeps the account's tables; the browser skips the first-name prompt when signed in and tags its table list.

Purpose: D-05 (the device token goes with the account) and D-12 (signing out gives the seats back), the base of ACCT-04's friends at a table and ACCT-02's cross-device list.
Output: accounts.ts, the GameRoom and router changes, members.ts, the workerd and e2e tests, the client list merge.
</objective>
