# Phase 4 Plan 07: « C’est ton tour » et les notifications quand l’application est fermée (Web Push)

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** avoir mon compte, retrouver mes amis et lancer une table ou une partie solo depuis l'application installée, **so that** je joue avec eux sans recopier de lien et je suis prévenu quand c'est mon tour ou qu'on m'invite.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal; see 04-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan, when Paul's phone locks during a game, the table pauses for everyone as before, and 30 s later his phone shows « C’est ton tour ! — La table de Romain t’attend » (or the card in the app if he has TOC open on another screen). An invitation to a table reaches him the same way when TOC is closed. The server chooses, the push service carries it, the service worker of 04-08 shows it; « Activer les notifications » is drawn in 04-10.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → subscription routes | A signed-in client chooses the endpoint the server will fetch |
| PlayerHub → push services | Outbound requests carrying encrypted payloads and a signed JWT |
| GameRoom → PlayerHub | Server-only notices about a table |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-53 | Elevation of privilege | server-side request forgery through the endpoint | high | mitigate | https only, host allow-list of the push services (test host only in TOC_TEST_MODE), length caps |
| T-04-54 | Information disclosure | push payloads (research V8) | medium | mitigate | aes128gcm end to end; payload holds the kind, a pseudo, the table link and a tag, never a hand, a card or a score |
| T-04-55 | Information disclosure | VAPID private key | high | mitigate | One Worker secret, put by the orchestrator through a pipe from tools/vapid-keys.mjs (never printed, never written); test pairs generated at test start, the e2e pair labelled test-only |
| T-04-56 | Information disclosure | stale or reassigned endpoints (research T-04-10) | medium | mitigate | Upsert by endpoint to the current account; delete on 404/410, on sign-out and on recovery (cascade) |
| T-04-57 | Denial of service | notification flooding (research T-04-09) | medium | mitigate | One turn notice per table and pause, invites deduplicated and rate-limited (04-06); at most 10 subscriptions per account |
| T-04-58 | Repudiation | Apple's permission revocation for invisible pushes (research Pitfall 1) | medium | mitigate | Presence decided on the server; every push is shown by the service worker (04-08) |
