# Phase 4 Plan 03: Un Durable Object par compte (migration v2), et retrouver son compte avec le code de secours

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** avoir mon compte, retrouver mes amis et lancer une table ou une partie solo depuis l'application installée, **so that** je joue avec eux sans recopier de lien et je suis prévenu quand c'est mon tour ou qu'on m'invite.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal; see 04-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan, when Paul loses his phone, he types his pseudo and his backup code on his new phone, creates a passkey there, and finds his account again; his old phone is signed out and he gets a new code. Someone guessing codes is locked out after five tries. The account's own Durable Object, PlayerHub, is created here because the recovery needs a strongly consistent attempt counter without a D1 write per failure (04-RESEARCH Pattern 3); 04-05 uses its rate limits for friend codes, 04-06 and 04-07 add presence, notices and push to it. This is the only Durable Object migration of the phase (v2).

Output: PlayerHub.ts, auth/recover.ts, the configs with v2, the workerd tests, the client calls.
