# Phase 4 Plan 09: Écrans — l’accueil avec le compte, « Se connecter », l’invitation complète et « Retrouver mon compte »

## Phase Goal

**As a** proche de Romain, **I want to** avoir mon compte, retrouver mes amis et lancer une table ou une partie solo depuis l'application installée, **so that** je joue avec eux sans recopier de lien et je suis prévenu quand c'est mon tour ou qu'on m'invite.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal; see 04-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan every way into an account is on screen as the UI-SPEC draws it: the home's small tiles, the sign-in sheet with its passkey, every case of an invitation link (expired, used, opened in WhatsApp's browser, opened by someone who already has an account), the backup-code screen that cannot be lost by a stray tap, and the recovery on a new phone.

<objective>
Draw the home's account row and merged list, the sign-in sheet, the full invitation route, the guarded backup-code screen and the recovery route, on the account client of 04-01 to 04-05, with their end-to-end specs.

Purpose: SC1 on screen (ACCT-01, ACCT-02) with the UI-SPEC copy, layouts and states.
Output: SmallTile states, SignInSheet, Invitation and Recover routes, flow.ts, four online specs.
</objective>
