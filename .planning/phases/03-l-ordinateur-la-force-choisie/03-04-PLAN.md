# Phase 3 Plan 04: Le Champion — il cherche sur les donnes qu'il peut imaginer, joue pour son équipe, et ne triche jamais

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** choisir la force de chaque place tenue par l'ordinateur, du Poulain (un coup légal au hasard) au Champion (un vrai jeu d'équipe), **so that** je joue contre un ordinateur à ma mesure, qui ne triche jamais et qui joue à un rythme naturel.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal; see 03-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan the Champion is « le plus fort possible » within the time cap: the shared evaluation of plan 03-02 plus a search over the deals it can imagine from what it has seen. The no-cheat guarantee is proven by a property test that shuffles the hidden hands, and the strength by the one 5-vs-4 ladder run of the phase (about 12 minutes). The search is counted in work units so that phase 2 can run it unchanged in a Durable Object, whose clock does not advance during computation.

<objective>
Give level 5 its search (D-04) behind the same createThinker, built on worlds sampled from the view only (D-10), add the one engine field a human would remember (the card passed to the partner), prove no-cheat for every level, measure decision time on fixed positions, set the level-5 budget, and prove 5 beats 4 « nettement » (D-07).

Purpose: SC2 (top step of the ladder), SC3 (team play at level 5), SC4 (no cheating), SC5 (bounded thinking).
Output: knowledge.ts, determinize.ts, search.ts, the level-5 thinker and budget, givenToPartner, noCheat.test.ts, level-5 teamplay cases, bench.ts, tools/bench-bots.mjs, the 5-vs-4 ladder record.
</objective>
