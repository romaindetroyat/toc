# Phase 3 Plan 01: Traceur — la force choisie au lancement, retenue, et jouée dans la partie

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** choisir la force de chaque place tenue par l'ordinateur, du Poulain (un coup légal au hasard) au Champion (un vrai jeu d'équipe), **so that** je joue contre un ordinateur à ma mesure, qui ne triche jamais et qui joue à un rythme naturel.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal and its five success criteria. The ROADMAP `**Goal**` line is not written in user-story form; run `/gsd-mvp-phase 3` to formalise it if wanted.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan Romain picks « Trotteur » for his partner on the launch screen, reloads and finds it still checked, taps « Jouer la partie », and his partner plays at level 2 in the real game: one pure bot entry point for the five levels, fed by the seat view only, chosen by the seat's level, through the existing controller. Levels 2 to 5 already play with the shared evaluation and their own noise; plan 03-02 completes the evaluation (risk, hand), the exchange card and the ladder proof; plan 03-04 gives level 5 its search; plan 03-03 lays the launch screen out on every screen; plan 03-05 replaces the fixed think delay.

## Scope Sanity Justification

`files_modified` lists 18 files. The tracer must cross every layer the phase touches (picker, settings, session and controller, the bot package, the engine's 7) in ONE `type="tracer"` task, or it would be horizontal planning. Seven of the files are tests or a test spec. The bot files are new and small (levels, evaluation, candidates, thinker); the web files get small additions; the engine gets one function in Task 2. The full launch layouts, the evaluation's risk and hand terms, the exchange choice, the search and the pacing are left to their own plans on purpose.
