# Phase 3 Plan 05: Le rythme naturel — réfléchir selon le coup, calcul compris, avance rapide, jamais plus de quelques secondes

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** choisir la force de chaque place tenue par l'ordinateur, du Poulain (un coup légal au hasard) au Champion (un vrai jeu d'équipe), **so that** je joue contre un ordinateur à ma mesure, qui ne triche jamais et qui joue à un rythme naturel.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal; see 03-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

Until now every bot thought a fixed 700 to 1300 ms, then computed its move. After this plan an obvious move comes quickly, a delicate one takes up to about 2 seconds, the search's own time is inside that wait, « Avance rapide » makes the computer play almost at once, and the Champion's search never freezes the screen or runs past the cap. The pace lives in the browser host; the bot only says how hard the decision was, so that phase 2 can pace the same bot with Durable Object alarms.

<objective>
Replace thinkDelay with a pure paceFor driven by the bot's difficulty (D-11, D-13), make the decision before the wait and count it inside, add the cap, the time-sliced inline runner and the stale-decision guard (BOT-05).

Purpose: SC5, « un temps de réflexion naturel … ne dépasse jamais quelques secondes ».
Output: pace.ts, botRunner.ts, the controller's new bot loop, rythme.spec.ts.
</objective>
