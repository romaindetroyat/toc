# Phase 3 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 3 « L’ordinateur à la force choisie ». Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Sources: `03-CONTEXT.md` (D-01..D-13, locked), `03-RESEARCH.md` (Patterns 8 and 9, Pitfall 1, open questions 1 and 2), the phase-1 contract `01-UI-SPEC.md` and its addenda (inherited in full), and the current code: `apps/web/src/routes/Launch.svelte`, `ui/Segmented.svelte`, `ui/SeatChip.svelte`, `ui/ColorTile.svelte`, `ui/InfoBar.svelte`, `ui/ActionZone.svelte`, `ui/screen.svelte.ts`, `app.css`, `settings/settings.ts`.
> This document is in English. Every user-facing string is French and quoted as it must appear. French typography rules of phase 1 apply in the catalogue (U+202F before `; ! ? »` and after `«`, U+00A0 before `:`, `’`, U+202F between a number and its unit and as the thousands separator).
> Items marked **(à confirmer par Romain)** are researcher choices inside Romain's decisions. They are collected in the last section and do not block planning.

**Scope (small, three surfaces only):**
1. The per-seat strength picker on the launch screen (D-01, D-02, D-03).
2. The discreet « thinking » sign on a computer player (D-12); made more visible on 2026-10-17 (plan 03-09, « Pas assez visible »), including a glow around the seat's rider on the board.
3. The bench page that times the computer on a real phone (03-RESEARCH Pitfall 1, « Real mid-range phone » fallback). It stays out of normal navigation.

Nothing else in the game screen, the board, the sheets or the end plank changes in this phase.

## Launch screen (D-01, D-02, D-03)

### What changes

- The visual team summary (« Ton équipe : » chips « + », « Adversaires : » chips) is replaced by the section « Force de l’ordinateur »: one row per computer seat, each row naming the seat and holding its picker. The `sr-only` team sentences stay.
- Row order: the partner, then the opponent on the human's left on the board, then the opponent on the right (`screenSideOf(seat, human)` in `packages/board/src/scene.ts`: left = next seat clockwise, right = previous seat).
- « Réglages » moves from under the button to the same row, left of « Jouer la partie »: one button row `[Réglages] 16 px [Jouer la partie, flex]`. DOM order = visual order.
- The launch screen gets explicit layouts per screen (below) so that it fits every target size; the colour tiles shrink in the compact layouts.
- The primary visual anchor stays the colour tiles (the only oak surfaces), then the single amber « Jouer la partie ».

### Strength row (`StrengthPicker.svelte`, new)

One row = an identity (small `SeatChip` with the colour name + role word) and a `Segmented` radio group of the five level names.

| Form | When (container query on the row, `container-type: inline-size`) | Structure | Height |
|------|-------------------|-----------|--------|
| Inline | row width ≥ 488 | identity block 96 wide (line 1: small `SeatChip` 20 high with the name; line 2: role word, Label 400 `--creme-attenuee`, 20 high; 4 gap), 8 gap, picker `flex: 1` (≥ 384) | 48 |
| Stacked | 384 ≤ row width < 488 | identity line 20 high (small chip, 8 gap, role word), 4 gap, picker full width | 72 |
| Narrow | row width < 384 (portrait phones only) | identity line, 4 gap, picker wrapped on two rows: « Poulain Trotteur Galopeur » then « Pur-sang Champion » (grid of 6 columns: spans 2-2-2 then 3-3), one bordered block | 116 |

Picker details:
- Reuses `Segmented.svelte` as is (WAI-ARIA radio group, one tab stop, arrow keys move and select, wrap around, `min-height: var(--touch)`). Options are equal width (`flex: 1 1 0`), `white-space: nowrap`.
- Horizontal option padding 4 px for this picker only (the sound-test picker keeps 8 px): `Segmented` gains an optional `--segmented-pad` custom property (default `var(--space-sm)`), set to `var(--space-xs)` by `StrengthPicker`. It also gains an optional per-option `title`, and a `wrap` mode for the narrow form. No other change to `Segmented`.
- Values: `Segmented` is generic over strings; the picker maps `BotLevel` 1..5 to `"1"`..`"5"` (or the planner widens the generic). The planner's call.
- A change is saved at once (no Save button) and applied to the next game; there is no change during a game.
- Test hooks: the row carries `data-strength-row="partner" | "left" | "right"`; the checked option is found by role (`getByRole("radio", { name: "Champion" })` inside the named group).

### Persistence (D-03)

- Stored in the existing settings object, key `toc.settings.v1`, field `botLevels: { partner, left, right }`, each validated by `parseBotLevel` and falling back to 3 independently (a missing, mistyped, out-of-range or string value such as `"5"` or `99` gives Galopeur). Unknown keys stay ignored, so older stored objects load.
- Memory is per role, not per colour **(à confirmer par Romain)**: « mon partenaire est Champion » survives a change of colour; the opponent on the left keeps the opponent-left level. When the player taps another colour tile, the rows' chips change colour at once and their levels stay.
- « Rejouer » (end plank) replays with the same levels; « Changer de couleur » returns to the launch screen showing the remembered levels.
- Storage unavailable: kept in memory for the session, no message (phase-1 rule).

| Colour | The ring takes the light tint of the seat (`--seat-{couleur}-clair`, the board's `PALETTE.light`: bleu `#5C86D4`, jaune `#F7D35A`, rouge `#EE6B5E`, vert `#5CC27A`, readable on bois sombre); the board glow takes `PALETTE.hex`. Never amber (amber is the own-turn dot) |
| Implementation | one shared utility in `app.css`: `.respire` (the animation and its ring, coloured by `--respire-seat` set on the element) and `.respire-mark` (the static « … », Label 14 / 700, `--creme-attenuee`, `aria-hidden`). Reduced motion (the phase-1 dual switch): when `settings.reduceMotion` is on (`class:still` on the component root) or `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` matches, `.respire` and its ring have `animation: none`, the ring stays at opacity 0.9, the board glow stays at 0.85, and `.respire-mark` is shown; otherwise `.respire-mark` is `display: none`. The marker, the ring and the glow appear and disappear without transition.

## Bench page « Banc d’essai de l’ordinateur » (03-RESEARCH Pitfall 1)

Purpose: Romain opens it on his iPhone during the human-verify step to see how long each level, mainly Champion, takes to choose a move on a real phone, and whether the screen freezes.

### Access

- Route `#/banc`, present in every build and linked from nowhere (not in Réglages, not on the launch screen, not in the help sheet) **(à confirmer par Romain)**. Its code is a lazy chunk (`import()` inside the route branch), so the normal app does not load it. Reason: Romain must be able to open it on his phone without a special debug build. It reads no game in progress and computes only on fixed positions. If the planner prefers a debug-only route (`__TOC_DEBUG__`, like `#/panne`), Romain's phone must then load a debug build.
- Not gated by « Tourne ton téléphone »: it works held either way.

### Layout

One column, max width 640, gutters 16 + insets, vertical padding 16, the page scrolls. Top to bottom:
1. Header row: « Retour » text button (≥ 44 × 44) at the left, title « Banc d’essai de l’ordinateur » (Heading) after it; on narrow screens the title wraps.
2. Intro (Body).
3. « Force mesurée » (Label 700) then the level picker: the same `Segmented` options as the launch screen (stacked form, no identity line), default « Champion » (not the stored launch levels).
4. Action row: « Lancer la mesure » (primary, 48 high, min 160 wide). While running it is replaced by « Arrêter la mesure » (secondary), and the picker is disabled (40 % opacity).
5. Progress (only while running): « Coup {i} sur {n} » (Label, tabular) and a progress bar `role="progressbar"` 8 high, radius 4, track `--bois-sombre`, fill `--creme`, full column width.
6. Result card (`--bois-sombre`, radius 16, padding 16; gap 24 above): before any run the empty state (heading « Aucune mesure pour l’instant » in Body 700, body in Body muted); after a run « Résultats » (Heading), then a two-column list (`<dl>`): label Label 400 `--creme-attenuee` at the left, value Label 700 tabular at the right, one row per measure, row height ≥ 24; then the verdict line (Body 700 with a 16 px `check` or `x` icon, cream); then « Copier les résultats » (secondary).

### What it measures

- The fixed bench positions shared with `tools/bench-bots.mjs` (the planner fixes the set, at least 30 decisions, including 7s with many outcomes).
- Each decision runs through the same inline time-sliced runner and the same per-level budget as the game, without the pacing delay and without the hard deadline, so the time shown is the real compute time on this device.
- « Temps médian » = p50, « 95 % des coups en moins de » = p95, « Le plus long » = max of the per-decision wall time; « Plus longue image figée » = the longest gap between two `requestAnimationFrame` callbacks during the run (shows whether time slicing keeps the screen alive).
- Verdict « Assez rapide sur cet appareil. » when p95 ≤ 1000 ms, max ≤ 2000 ms and the longest frozen frame ≤ 100 ms; otherwise « Trop lent sur cet appareil. Envoie ces résultats. » **(seuils à confirmer par Romain)**.
- « Arrêter la mesure » keeps the decisions already measured and shows their results under « Mesure arrêtée après {i} coups. ».
- If the page becomes hidden during a run, the run stops and shows the « Mesure interrompue… » line; results of an interrupted run are not judged (no verdict).

### The phone held sideways (compact mode): the hand in the corner

Measured before building, from `packages/board` (insets 0): the galet’s bare bottom-left corner is a quarter disc of about 110 px from the screen corner, and along the left strip the ring’s cells stand 2 to 7 px right of the board’s edge. Whole 80 px cards cannot stand there without covering cells. So the hand is a cascade of large cards from the bottom of the left strip up, each pushed as far right as the scene geometry allows (the lower ones lean into the corner), and running past the screen’s left edge when the strip is narrower than the card. On an iPhone the safe-area inset (about 47 px each side in landscape) gives the cards that room first, so they show (nearly) whole there.
