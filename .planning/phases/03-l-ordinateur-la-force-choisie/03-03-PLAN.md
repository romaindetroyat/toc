# Phase 3 Plan 03: Le lancement sur tous les écrans — les trois lignes de force, deux colonnes, la bande du bouton

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** choisir la force de chaque place tenue par l'ordinateur, du Poulain (un coup légal au hasard) au Champion (un vrai jeu d'équipe), **so that** je joue contre un ordinateur à ma mesure, qui ne triche jamais et qui joue à un rythme naturel.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal; see 03-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

Plan 03-01 put the three strength rows in the phase-1 launch column. This plan lays the launch screen out as the UI-SPEC « Launch screen layouts » says, so that the colour, the three levels and « Jouer la partie » fit on the computer and on the phone held sideways without scrolling, and stay usable on very small and upright screens. It changes only the launch screen and its components.

<objective>
Implement the 03-UI-SPEC launch contract (D-01 surfaces): the strength row's three forms, the additive Segmented options, the four layouts with the sticky button band, the compact colour tiles, and the end-to-end fit checks at the target sizes.

Purpose: SC1 on Romain's real devices: the choice must be easy on the phone held sideways and on the computer.
Output: StrengthPicker forms, Segmented additions, Launch layouts, ColorTile compact, launch.spec.ts and force.spec.ts fit cases.
</objective>
