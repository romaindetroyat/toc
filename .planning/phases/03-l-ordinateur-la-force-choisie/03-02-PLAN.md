# Phase 3 Plan 02: Les niveaux 2 à 4 — le jeu d'équipe, des erreurs de moins en moins, la carte d'échange, et l'échelle qui le prouve

## Phase Goal

**As a** joueur, **I want to** choisir la force de chaque place tenue par l'ordinateur, du Poulain (un coup légal au hasard) au Champion (un vrai jeu d'équipe), **so that** je joue contre un ordinateur à ma mesure, qui ne triche jamais et qui joue à un rythme naturel.

(Mapped from the ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal; see 03-01.)

## This Plan's Slice

After this plan the levels 2, 3 and 4 are the real Trotteur, Galopeur and Pur-sang: they share one evaluation that weighs rentrer, manger, se protéger and aider le partenaire, they pass a sensible card to the partner, and a ladder tool proves on 400 paired games that each beats the one below « nettement ». Level 5 stays the noiseless policy until plan 03-04 gives it its search; the 5-vs-4 run lives there, and only there.

<objective>
Build the ladder first (the measuring tool), then complete the team evaluation and the exchange card, then tune the levels until 2 → 1, 3 → 2 and 4 → 3 pass the stated bar.

Purpose: D-05, D-07, D-08 and D-09 are judged on numbers and positions, not on feel; the tool also protects every later change (research Pitfall 7).
Output: match.ts, tools/ladder.mjs, the ladder smoke test, the risk and hand terms of evaluate.ts, exchange.ts, teamplay.test.ts, tuned levels.ts.
</objective>
